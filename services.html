Thermoplastic Pavement Markings

When you need markings that stand up to heavy traffic and the elements, thermoplastic is the answer. Heat-applied and thicker than paint, it bonds directly to the asphalt for crosswalks, symbols, arrows, and high-wear areas that stay bright and reflective for years. It's the go-to choice for schools, municipalities, and busy commercial entrances where durability and visibility matter most.

  • Long-lasting crosswalks & stop bars
  • Handicap symbols & directional arrows
  • Reflective options for nighttime visibility
  • Ideal for high-traffic and municipal work
