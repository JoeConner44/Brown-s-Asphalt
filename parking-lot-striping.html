Line Striping & Layout

Parking Lot Striping That Guides & Protects

Sharp, high-visibility lines do more than look good — they direct traffic safely, maximize your usable spaces, and keep your property compliant.

Faded, crooked, or missing lines send the wrong message to every customer who pulls in — and can expose you to real liability. BAM Striping restores order to your lot with clean, durable striping applied by an experienced crew that's been doing this across North Georgia for more than 25 years.

What We Stripe

  • Standard & compact parking stalls
  • ADA-accessible spaces & access aisles
  • Fire lanes & no-parking zones
  • Directional arrows & traffic flow markings
  • Loading zones, curbing & wheel-stop lines
  • Custom stencils: "Reserved," "Stop," handicap symbols, numbers

New Layouts & Re-Striping

Already have a lot? We re-stripe right over your existing pattern to bring it back to life — ideally paired with fresh sealcoat for a like-new finish. Starting from scratch or reconfiguring? We design efficient layouts that fit more compliant spaces into the same footprint while keeping traffic flowing safely.

Built to Last

We use high-quality, fast-drying traffic paints (and thermoplastic where longevity matters most) applied at the right thickness for maximum durability. The result is crisp edges and bright lines that hold up to weather and traffic far longer than a rushed, thin coat.

Why property owners choose BAM for striping

  • Straight, professional lines every time — no wobble, no overspray
  • ADA & fire-code compliant layouts done right the first time
  • After-hours & weekend scheduling to avoid disrupting your business
  • Clear, written estimates with no hidden fees

Parking Lot Striping FAQs

How much does parking lot striping cost? +

Cost depends on lot size, number of stalls, and whether it's a re-stripe or a fresh layout. We provide free, itemized written estimates so you know exactly what you're paying for.

How long does the paint last? +

Quality traffic paint typically lasts 18–24 months depending on traffic, weather, and whether the lot has been sealcoated. High-traffic lots may need annual re-striping.

Do you handle ADA-compliant striping? +

Yes. We lay out accessible stalls, access aisles, and signage to meet current ADA and local code requirements.
