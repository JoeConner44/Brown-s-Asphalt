Our Story

Built on 40 Years of Asphalt Heritage

Brown's Asphalt Maintenance grew out of a family paving business — and that hands-on legacy shows in every line we lay down across North Georgia.

Who We Are

A Family Name You Can Trust

Ken Brown started BAM in the shadow of his father's paving business while still a teenager. Four decades later, that same commitment to doing the job right — the first time — still drives every crew we send out.

We're not a franchise or a faceless national chain. We're a locally owned company based in Winterville, right outside Athens, and we treat every property like it's on our own street. When you call BAM, you talk to people who actually do the work and stand behind it.
