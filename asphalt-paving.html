Repair & Install

Asphalt Paving & Repair Done Right

From nagging potholes to full-depth failures, we fix the problems that make a lot look neglected — and keep them from coming back.

Potholes and cracks aren't just an eyesore — they're a trip hazard, a liability, and a sign of deeper damage spreading beneath the surface. With roots in a family paving business, BAM brings real asphalt know-how to every repair, big or small.

Our Asphalt Services

  • Pothole repair & patching
  • Crack filling & sealing
  • Asphalt overlays & resurfacing
  • Full-depth repair of failed sections
  • New asphalt installation for lots & drives
  • Drainage & grading corrections

Patch & Repair

For localized damage, we cut out the failed area, prep a solid base, and install fresh hot-mix asphalt compacted to last. Done properly, a patch blends in and holds up — instead of crumbling again after the first hard rain.

Overlays & Resurfacing

When the base is still sound but the surface is worn, an overlay gives you a smooth, new driving surface at a fraction of the cost of full replacement — the ideal reset before sealcoating and fresh striping.

New Installation

Adding a lot, an approach, or expanding your footprint? We handle grading, base prep, and paving so the finished surface drains correctly and stands up to daily commercial traffic.

The smart sequence

For the best value and longest life, asphalt work usually follows a natural order: repair → overlay (if needed) → sealcoat → stripe. Because we do all four, we can plan the whole project so each step supports the next — and you only coordinate with one crew.
