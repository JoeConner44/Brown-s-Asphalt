Sealcoating That Extends Pavement Life

A fresh sealcoat is the cheapest insurance your asphalt can buy — locking out the water, sun, and oil that turn small cracks into expensive repairs.

Asphalt is a big investment, and Georgia's sun and rain work against it every single day. Sealcoating adds a protective barrier that shields the surface, slows oxidation, and instantly restores that deep, uniform black look that tells customers you care about your property.

What Sealcoating Does for You

  • Blocks UV rays that dry out and crack asphalt
  • Repels water, oil, and gas that break down the binder
  • Restores a rich, uniform black finish
  • Makes future sweeping and cleaning easier
  • Extends pavement life and delays costly resurfacing
  • Creates the perfect base for crisp new striping

Our Sealcoating Process

Great sealcoat is all about prep. We clean the surface thoroughly, address oil spots, and fill cracks first — because sealer applied over a dirty or cracked lot simply won't bond or last. Then we apply a quality commercial-grade sealer at the correct rate for even coverage and lasting protection.

Crack filling included where it counts

Cracks are how water gets underneath your pavement and destroys the base. We hot-pour fill working cracks as part of a proper maintenance plan so your sealcoat protects a sound surface — not a failing one.

Pair it with striping

The best time to re-stripe is right after sealcoating. We handle both, so your lot goes from tired and gray to sharp, black, and clearly marked in a single coordinated visit.

Sealcoating FAQs

How often should I sealcoat? +

Most commercial lots should be sealcoated every 2–3 years. New asphalt should cure 6–12 months before its first coat.

How long before I can drive on it? +

Usually a few hours for foot traffic and 24–48 hours for vehicles, depending on temperature and humidity. We plan around your schedule.
