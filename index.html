Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a parking lot be re-striped? +

Most commercial lots benefit from fresh striping every 18–24 months, though high-traffic properties may need it annually. Faded lines aren't just unattractive — they can create liability and ADA-compliance issues.

How long does sealcoating take to dry? +

Sealcoat typically cures enough for foot traffic in a few hours and vehicle traffic within 24–48 hours, depending on temperature and humidity. We schedule around your business to minimize downtime.

Do you offer free estimates? +

Yes — every estimate is free and comes with no obligation. We visit your property, measure the work, and provide a clear written quote.

Can you work after hours or on weekends? +

Absolutely. For retail, medical, and office properties we regularly schedule evening and weekend work so your customers and staff are never disrupted.

Are you licensed and insured? +

Yes. Brown's Asphalt Maintenance is fully insured, and every layout we stripe is built to meet ADA and local fire-lane requirements.
